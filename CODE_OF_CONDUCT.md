# Code of conduct

We pledge to make participation in Veriplane a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Contributors should demonstrate empathy, respect differing viewpoints, give and accept constructive feedback, take responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, sexualized conduct, trolling, insults, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate these standards, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for inappropriate behavior. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of reporters.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
